Lya Chiñas

PhD @ UofT | AI + Biology

I'm a 3rd year PhD student in the Laboratory for Metabolic Systems Engineering at the University of Toronto.

I build models that explain cell behavior from genetic information. It's a two-stage process: machine learning predicts properties from DNA and protein sequences, and metabolic models use those predictions to simulate how cells grow and respond. The goal is to speed up biotechnology, such as designing microbes that produce fuels, medicines, and materials.

In addition to research, I enjoy sharing this knowledge as a Teaching Assistant for the graduate courses CHE1147 Chemical Data Science and Engineering and CHE118 Artificial Intelligence for Applied Chemistry and Chemical Engineering.

Coding aside, fitness is a big passion of mine! I’ve been weightlifting for over six years, and my latest race was the La Jolla Half Marathon.
